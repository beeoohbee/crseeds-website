Who is CR?

John “Cory Robinson” developed CR Seeds & Services LLC in 2014. But it all started back in 2003 after having seed production internships where the passion for agronomy was born. While focusing on crop scouting; CR was also able to experience seed sales management too. It was here where CR learned the value tying the two areas together could provide the greatest service to local farmers. After graduating from University of Missouri-Columbia Cum Laude with a Bachelors Degree in Agribusiness Management and minor in Agriculture Systems Management; CR became a District Sales Manager for Syngenta/Golden Harvest in SW MO, SE KS and NE OK. Later moved into the role of Account Manager with Corteva/Pioneer in West Central MO. Through this time of recruiting/growing sales with network of independent sales representatives; CR wanted to become an independent rep of his own. Family was growing and wanted to get back to home area before the oldest child started Kindergarten. Warehouse completed by 2015 to distribute Pioneer Hybrids. By 2016; CR also became Area Manager of Maximum Farming System by Ag Spectrum. Then in 2022 made seed distribution change to Beck’s Superior Hybrids. CR is focused on working along the side of Farmers to provide the most value to their operation/family by serving, guiding and providing insights between soil, seed and crop management.
